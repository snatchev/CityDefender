# City flavor: events, power-ups, debuffs and landmarks

> Research notes, 2026-09-27. Stefan's request: every city gets **location-appropriate special events, power-ups and debuffs**, and its map is full of **beloved, popular and locally hated landmarks and signs**. Research first, build later. Backlog entry: [IDEAS.md](../IDEAS.md#city-flavor-events-power-ups-debuffs-and-landmarks-stefan-2026-09-27).

**Method and limits.** Stefan asked for non-traditional sources such as Reddit. Reddit blocks the research tools used here (both `reddit.com` and `old.reddit.com` refuse fetches), so this leans on the sources closest to that voice:
- local alt-weeklies and neighbourhood news: Billy Penn, PhillyVoice, Universal Hub, Boston.com reader polls, Gothamist, SFGate, SF Standard, Willamette Week,
- meme explainers such as Billy Penn's guide to the "Philly iceberg".

Each item is either **sourced** (linked at the end) or marked *(general knowledge, verify)*. Worth a pass by a local, or a read of the subreddits, before anything ships.

---

## 1. How city flavor plugs into the game

Every idea below maps onto one of five mechanic types, and most reuse systems the sim already has.

| Type | What it is | Built from |
|---|---|---|
| **Event** (good, bad or mixed) | Something that happens to the city for a wave. Announced on the radio during prep, so it's never a surprise. Seeded, so a seed replays it. About one every 3 waves. | Flow-field extra cost (crowds, closures), temporary or moving barricades, slows, station open/close, spawn changes, economy. |
| **Power-up** | A local ally or trick the player fires once, with limited charges. Earned from side objectives or picked as a Council Grant (Pass 9). | Area damage or knockback, slows, stuns, temporary barricades, cash. |
| **Debuff** | A local nuisance that works against the player. | The same levers aimed the other way: barricades removed, range cut, towers slowed, costs up. |
| **Landmark** | A named building or sign with a small rule of its own (a range beacon, a curse, a shortcut). Hated landmarks get comedic downsides. | Per-footprint tags from the map pipeline, special roof pads, info cards. |
| **Voice** | Radio chatter, station names, the local mascot boss. | Text lines, names; no mechanics. |

This extends two things already in the plan: **Council Grants** (Pass 9: pick 1 of 3 every 5 waves) and **mutators** (Pass 11 already lists Rush Hour, SEPTA Strike, Nor'easter, Construction Season and Budget Cuts). Each city would ship a deck of events, a pool of city-flavoured grants and power-ups, and a few city mutators.

**Balance rule of thumb:** a mixed event beats a pure buff or pure nerf. "The street is packed with celebrating fans: bugs crawl through them slowly, but your towers can't fire into the crowd" is a decision; "+20% damage" is not.

## 2. Philadelphia (level 1: Center City, defend City Hall)

### Events
- **Broad Street Bullies** *(Stefan's idea; good)*: a squad of 1970s-style hockey enforcers skates down Broad Street, body-checking every bug on it (knockback plus damage along the whole avenue), then leaves. The Flyers' "Broad Street Bullies" nickname was coined by a Bulletin sportswriter in 1973 ([Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/flyers/broad-street-bullies-nickname-history-philadelphia-bulletin-20240515.html)). The nickname is tied to the Flyers brand, so ship an original name ("the Bullies of Broad") and unbranded jerseys.
- **Wheelie Boys** *(Stefan's idea; bad)*: a dirt-bike and ATV pack rips through during prep, wheelies around City Hall and smashes one or two barricades on its path. Wheelies around City Hall on Broad Street are a real, viral and much-policed thing; the city seized 680+ bikes in 2024 ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/philadelphia/2025/02/26/atvs-illegal-dirt-bikes-philly-police-crackdown), [CBS](https://www.cbsnews.com/philadelphia/news/philadelphia-illegal-atvs-dirt-bikes-city-streets/)). Counterplay: a bike's path is telegraphed and Jersey Barriers survive it.
- **Go Birds! / Grease the Poles** *(mixed)*: after a big win, fans flood Broad Street and climb light poles, SEPTA entrances and trucks. The city greased poles with Crisco from 2009, switched to hydraulic fluid in 2018 and skipped greasing in 2025 ([Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/news/philadelphia/poles-grease-eagles-fans-philadelphia-new-orleans-20250209.html), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/philadelphia/2025/02/10/eagles-super-bowl-win-philly-broad-street-poles)). In game, the streets around City Hall fill with a crowd: a huge path cost for bugs, but towers can't shoot into crowd tiles. Power-up variant **Grease the Poles**: for one wave, climbing bugs can't scale roof pads (for Pass 8's climbers and spitters).
- **Concrete Cowboys** *(good)*: the Fletcher Street urban riders trot a lane, trampling small bugs. They're on the Philly iceberg as a local icon ([Billy Penn](https://billypenn.com/2021/04/19/philadelphia-iceberg-meme-explained-guide-cameron-jones/)). They're a real community, so portray them respectfully and heroically.
- **Mummers Strut** *(mixed; New Year's wave)*: a slow parade fills Broad as a moving barricade. The Mummers Parade is the oldest folk parade in the country ([Lonely Planet](https://www.lonelyplanet.com/articles/things-to-know-before-traveling-to-philadelphia)). Note: the parade has a documented blackface controversy (on the iceberg), so keep the skins to feathers and sequins.
- **Savesies** *(good)*: residents "save" parking spots with traffic cones and lawn chairs. Free weak barricades appear on side streets ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/philadelphia/2025/12/22/philly-imaginary-cheesesteak-factory-best-thing-reddit-gave-us-in-2025)).
- **SEPTA trouble** *(mixed)*: "Trolley tunnel closed" keeps one spawn station shut for a wave (good); "Regional Rail delays" makes your next build arrive late (bad). The Center City trolley tunnel really was shut for months after a failed repair ([Triangle](https://www.thetriangle.org/news/septa-trolley-tunnel-reopens-after-monthslong-shutdown)). Pass 11 already plans a "SEPTA Strike" mutator.

### Power-ups
- **Wooder Ice**: a Cryo burst over a whole block (slow and brain-freeze stun). Water ice is a Philly staple ([PA Eats](https://www.paeats.org/feature/pa-food-icons-water-ice/)).
- **The Orange Menace**: a furry, googly-eyed agent of chaos runs through the level, and bugs scatter from him (fear: they reroute away from his tile). It's an homage to Gritty, "unhinged chaotic good" ([Billy Penn](https://billypenn.com/2021/04/19/philadelphia-iceberg-meme-explained-guide-cameron-jones/)). **Must be an original character**: Gritty and the Phanatic are team IP, and the Phanatic has been through a copyright lawsuit ([Inquirer](https://www.inquirer.com/opinion/commentary/phillies-phanatic-copyright-lawsuit-gritty-20190830.html)).
- **Hot-dog cannon**: a mascot-style cannon on a roof; a one-shot splash. Fits the city's two famous mascots ([City Cast Philly](https://philly.citycast.fm/explainers/the-lore-of-philly-s-mascots)).
- **Wanamaker Organ**: once per run, the world's largest concert organ (inside Macy's in Center City) booms and stuns every bug in a radius ([Billy Penn](https://billypenn.com/2021/04/19/philadelphia-iceberg-meme-explained-guide-cameron-jones/)). The building is inside the level.
- **Toynbee Tiles** *(collectible)*: tiles hidden in the asphalt, densest on Chestnut Street south of Dilworth Park ([Atlas Obscura](https://www.atlasobscura.com/articles/toynbee-tiles), [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toynbee_tiles)). Click one to find it; five found lets you "RESURRECT DEAD" one destroyed barricade.

### Debuffs
- **The Parking Authority**: a tow truck removes one of your barricades during prep. The PPA is famously hated, and it had its own reality show, *Parking Wars* *(general knowledge, verify)*.
- **Mister Softee**: the truck parks mid-block and blocks the lane until it's done; its jingle distracts gunners (fire rate down nearby). The trucks started in Philadelphia in 1956, and locals have written "Mister Softee, Please Shut Up" ([Philadelphia Citizen](https://thephiladelphiacitizen.org/mister-softee-please-shut-up/), [PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/philadelphia-native-and-creator-beloved-mister-softee-ice-cream-truck-jingle-has-died/)). Trademarked name and jingle, so use a generic ice-cream truck with an original jingle.

### Landmarks (all inside or next to the level)
- **Beloved:**
  - City Hall and the **William Penn** statue (the goal).
  - **LOVE** statue at LOVE Park.
  - Oldenburg's **Clothespin**.
  - The **PSFS** neon sign, red at night and visible for 20 miles ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/PSFS_Building)).
  - Reading Terminal Market's neon sign.
  - The Comcast Center.
  - The Masonic Temple.
- **The Billy Penn Curse** *(landmark rule)*: nothing was built taller than William Penn's hat until One Liberty Place (1987), and a sports drought followed. It was "lifted" when builders put a mini Billy Penn statue on the Comcast Center's top beam ([Billy Penn](https://billypenn.com/2021/04/19/philadelphia-iceberg-meme-explained-guide-cameron-jones/), [Metro](https://metrophiladelphia.com/stories/philadelphia-250-curses-superstitions-tradition,144715)). Game rule: towers on pads above City Hall's hat have a small miss chance, **unless** you place a mini Billy Penn (a one-off grant) on that roof, which turns it into a blessing (+range).
- **Hated:**
  - The **Aramark Tower** (1101 Market), voted Pennsylvania's ugliest building.
  - The **Municipal Services Building**, on a list of the ugliest US public buildings.
  - The windowless **Mint** ([Patch](https://patch.com/pennsylvania/bensalem/really-pennsylvanias-ugliest-building), [Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/philadelphia/2022/08/26/philadelphia-eyesores-ugly-buildings)).

  Joke rule: roof pads on these cost less ("nobody wants to be up there").
- **The Disney Hole**: the lot at 8th and Market left empty by a failed 1998 Disney theme-park plan ([Billy Penn](https://billypenn.com/2021/04/19/philadelphia-iceberg-meme-explained-guide-cameron-jones/)). It sits on the level's east edge, so make it an open lot bugs can cut across.

### Voice
Radio lines in local speech:
- "Somebody grab that jawn!"
- "They're coming up Chestnut past the Wawa!"
- "Yo, they're on Broad!"

"Jawn" is now in Dictionary.com ([PhillyVoice](https://www.phillyvoice.com/jawn-philly-slang-dictionary-entry/)). Wawa vs Sheetz is a live culture war ([Crossing Broad](https://www.crossingbroad.com/2022/11/billy-penn-survey-sheetz-should-move-into-abandoned-philly-wawa-locations.html)), which makes a good joke line, but brand names in art need care (§7).

## 3. Boston (suggested: Downtown and Beacon Hill, defend the State House's gold dome)

### Events
- **Storrowed** *(mixed)*: a moving truck wedges under a low bridge and blocks the street for two waves. That could be your route, or theirs. Boston has 100+ truck-vs-bridge crashes on the river roads (2022–25), peaking on September 1 ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/08/31/metro/storrowing-trucks-hit-overpasses-in-boston/), [Mass.gov](https://www.mass.gov/news/dcr-warns-drivers-about-storrowing-ahead-of-greater-boston-move-in-day)).
- **Allston Christmas** *(good; the September wave)*: the city-wide lease turnover leaves couches and IKEA shelves on every curb ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moving_Day_(Boston))). Free "couch barricades" everywhere, weak but many.
- **Nor'easter plus Space Savers** *(mixed)*: snow slows everyone. Afterwards residents claim shovelled spots with lawn chairs, cones and even beer kegs, legal for 48 hours after a snow emergency ([Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/cars/south-boston/2015/01/22/bostons-space-saving-tradition-explained/), [WBUR](https://www.wbur.org/news/2015/01/28/space-savers-boston)). In game they're free barricades that vanish after 2 waves. Pass 11 already plans a "Nor'easter" mutator.
- **Cue the Duck Boats** *(good, rare)*: a championship parade of amphibious duck boats loops downtown, crushing bugs on its route, but no building on parade streets. "Cue the Duck Boats" is a local catchphrase ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Boston_Duck_Tours)).
- **Masshole Rotary** *(bad)*: a random intersection seizes up in traffic, and its path cost spikes for everyone, including your preferred route. "Masshole" is in the Oxford dictionary; Boston.com readers ranked the "circles of death" rotaries ([Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/community/readers-say/2021/08/20/10-most-painful-rotaries-in-greater-boston/)).
- **The T** *(mixed)*:
  - "Slow zone": bugs from one station creep for a wave. The MBTA's slowness helps you for once.
  - "Shuttle buses replace service": bugs pop out at surprise shuttle stops. *(Leave out the 2022 Orange Line fire, where a rider jumped into the Mystic River; people were hurt and scared.)*
- **Wild turkeys** *(neutral chaos)*: aggressive turkeys wander the streets, pecking bugs and your street-level towers alike. Brookline even put up turkey sculptures ([Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/news/local-news/2024/02/25/brookline-to-honor-their-wild-turkey-friends-with-10-new-sculptures/)).

### Power-ups
- **One if by Land**: a lantern at the Old North Church reveals the next wave's stations and routes early (a wave-intel upgrade) *(general knowledge)*.
- **Tea Party**: bugs on waterfront tiles get dumped in the harbour.
- **Make Way for Ducklings**: a mother duck and her ducklings cross the street, and every bug on that street stops for 5 seconds. The statue is in the Public Garden ([Secret Boston](https://secretboston.co/boston-most-iconic-signs-history/)).
- **Iced-coffee run**: +50% fire rate for a wave. "Dunkies" is Boston's unofficial fuel; the Dunkin' name is trademarked, so keep the cup generic.
- **Big Dig** *(Construction Season mutator)*: one street is closed for the whole run and costs 10× the budget to finish *(general knowledge)*.

### Landmarks
- **Beloved:**
  - The State House.
  - The **Citgo sign** over Kenmore Square (since 1940, saved by locals, now protected).
  - The 40 ft **Hood Milk Bottle** outside the Children's Museum ([Secret Boston](https://secretboston.co/boston-most-iconic-signs-history/)).
  - The Old North Church.
  - The Custom House tower.
  - Fenway's Green Monster: could be a barricade skin ("Monster Wall").

  The Citgo sign sits outside a downtown level, so use it as a skyline marker in the backdrop.
- **Hated:**
  - **Boston City Hall**: brutalism, voted 4th ugliest building in the world, though Mayor Wu "will fight anyone" who disagrees ([Boston.com](https://www.boston.com/community/local-news/boston-readers-city-hall-ugliest-building/), [Harvard Crimson](https://www.thecrimson.com/article/2024/3/5/boston-city-hall-ugly-building-architecture-brutal-concrete-metro/)).
  - The Government Service Center, which "most people manage to ignore" ([Universal Hub](https://www.universalhub.com/2018/our-brutalist-tower-never-was)).

  Joke rule: City Hall Plaza is so bleak that bugs cross it slowly.
- **The Hancock Tower's falling windows** *(general knowledge, verify)*: in the 1970s its glass panes fell out and were replaced with plywood. Debuff: a "plywood palace" pad where towers occasionally lose accuracy.

**Skip:** the Molasses Flood (21 dead in 1919) and anything around the Marathon finish line (the 2013 bombing).

## 4. New York City (suggested: Midtown, defend the Empire State Building; the Flatiron is the IDEAS alternative)

### Events
- **Pizza Rat** *(mixed)*: rats swarm the streets eating bug corpses, so you lose those bounties, but rats also chew small bugs. Pizza Rat (2015, First Ave L station) is the canonical NYC meme ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pizza_Rat)).
- **Trash Revolution** *(mixed)*: garbage-bag walls line the sidewalks. "Empire Bins" containerization cuts the clutter but costs a wave's cash. This is the real anti-rat programme; the city even had a "rat czar" ([NYC.gov](https://www.nyc.gov/mayors-office/news/2025/06/the-empire-bin-strikes-back-against-rats-next-phase-trash-revolution-begins-west), [Audacy](https://www.audacy.com/1010wins/news/local/trash-bins-are-turning-the-tide-in-nyc-s-war-on-rats)).
- **Steam Pipe Blowout** *(bad, or a hazard both ways)*: a Con Ed steam line bursts, and its orange-and-white stack vents a scalding cloud that damages everything on the tile. Manhole explosions become surprise surfacing points for diggers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_York_City_steam_system), [Patch](https://patch.com/new-york/chelsea-ny/steam-pipe-explosion-flatiron-district-evacuates-builldings)).
- **Parade Balloons** *(good)*: giant character balloons drift down a parade avenue at roof height, blocking fliers such as Pass 8's wasps. Macy's balloons have a long history of mishaps ([Untapped New York](https://www.untappedcities.com/balloon-mishaps-macys-nyc-thanksgiving-day-parade/)); keep the characters original.
- **Showtime!** *(good)*: subway performers hit a station exit, and bugs leaving it stop to watch for a few seconds ([Voyage NY](https://voyageny.com/from-pizza-rats-to-rooftop-pigeon-coops-only-in-new-york-moments/)).
- **Delivery e-bike swarm** *(neutral)*: fast riders on every avenue knock small bugs aside, and they also nick barricades. E-bikes are a top local complaint ([amNY](https://www.amny.com/new-york/manhattan/central-park-bikes-e-bikes-battle/), [Crain's](https://www.crainsnewyork.com/transportation/city-launches-long-delayed-office-regulate-e-bike-delivery-boom)).
- **Congestion Pricing** *(mixed mutator)*: south of 60th Street, building costs +20%, but traffic is lighter, so every "jam" event is disabled there. Honking complaints in the zone fell 69% ([The City Reporter](https://www.thecityreporter.nyc/2025/03/11/traffic-noise-complaints-drop-congestion-pricing/)).

### Power-ups
- **Bodega Cat**: a cat on a corner shop's street-level spot pounces on small bugs.
- **Dollar Slice**: a cheap, temporary tower that lasts one wave.
- **Times Square Billboards**: a lure. For one wave the billboards attract bugs toward a chosen intersection (pull them into your kill zone).
- **Fire trucks**: a hose knockback down one avenue *(general knowledge)*.

### Landmarks
- **Beloved:**
  - The Empire State Building (the goal; its tower lights show wave status).
  - Chrysler Building (spire pad with top range).
  - Flatiron.
  - Grand Central (a station).
  - Across the river, the **Pepsi-Cola sign** (Queens) and the **Domino Sugar sign** (Brooklyn) as backdrop beacons ([Untapped New York](https://www.untappedcities.com/new-york-city-signs/)).
- **Hated:**
  - **Penn Station**, NYC's "hell on earth". A bug spawn, and bugs get lost inside so they leave late ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/news/new-yorkers-agree-that-this-is-the-worst-place-in-the-city-070820)).
  - **The Vessel**, the "shawarma" of Hudson Yards, the "Dubai of New York" ([Fast Company](https://www.fastcompany.com/90326416/why-everyone-hates-the-vessel)).
  - The **MetLife Building**, which blocks the Park Avenue view ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/newyork/things-to-do/the-ugliest-buildings-in-nyc)).
- Supertall "pencil towers": the highest pads in the game, but they sway (accuracy penalty) *(general knowledge)*.

**Skip:** real street personalities (the Naked Cowboy and the like); sanitation workers as the butt of jokes.

## 5. San Francisco (suggested: Downtown, SoMa and North Beach, defend the Transamerica Pyramid or the Ferry Building)

San Francisco's special twist is **hills**: slopes slow bugs uphill and speed them downhill, and cable cars rumble down the steepest streets as moving hazards. This needs elevation in the tile map (a map-pipeline feature, *not built yet*).

### Events
- **Karl the Fog** *(mixed)*: Karl, the fog with his own social accounts since 2010, rolls in and halves tower range. Bugs lose track of their routes too, so they wander (random tie-breaks go wild) ([KQED](https://www.kqed.org/news/11682057/how-the-bay-areas-fog-came-to-be-named-karl), [Bold Italic](https://www.thebolditalic.com/when-did-karl-the-fog-become-a-thing/)).
- **Robotaxi Coning** *(good)*: driverless cars roam as neutral traffic. Put a traffic cone on one and it freezes with hazards on, which makes it a free barricade. Activists really did this in the "Week of Cone" in 2023 ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2023/07/07/we-spoke-to-one-of-the-activists-coning-cruise-and-waymo-robotaxis-in-san-francisco/), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/06/robotaxi-haters-in-san-francisco-are-disabling-waymo-cruise-traffic-cones/)). Keep the car design generic, not Waymo's.
- **Sideshow** *(bad)*: cars spin donuts at an intersection, blocking it and damaging nearby barricades. Real sideshows have closed Market and Castro and stalled the 24-Divisadero bus ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2024/06/13/sideshows-sfpd-enforcement-stunt-driving/), [SFist](https://sfist.com/sideshows/)).
- **Earthquake** *(bad, rare)*: every barricade takes damage. The **Millennium Tower** (the "Leaning Tower of SoMa", sank 18 inches and tilts 14) leans a bit more, and towers on it lose accuracy ([SF Standard](https://sfstandard.com/2023/06/21/san-franciscos-infamous-leaning-tower-has-been-fixed/), [SFGate](https://www.sfgate.com/news/editorspicks/article/SF-s-sinking-Millenium-Tower-to-finally-get-fixed-15439863.php)).
- **Bay to Breakers** *(mixed)*: costumed runners flood one street as a moving crowd *(general knowledge)*. Salmon runners going the wrong way push bugs backwards.
- **Sea lions** *(good)*: waterfront tiles near Pier 39 bark bugs back from the Embarcadero.

### Power-ups
- **Imperial Decree**: once per run, Emperor Norton, the bankrupt merchant who declared himself Emperor of the United States in 1859 and was humoured by the city for 21 years ([FoundSF](https://www.foundsf.org/Emperor_Norton)), decrees a law. Pick one: towers cost half for a wave, or a new barricade tier for free.
- **Bummer and Lazarus**: two legendary stray dogs of the 1860s roam and catch small bugs ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bummer_and_Lazarus)).
- **Wild parrots**: the Telegraph Hill flock (SF's official animal) harasses fliers ([SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/bayarea/article/official-animal-wild-parrot-17869732.php)).

### Landmarks
- **Beloved:**
  - Transamerica Pyramid.
  - Coit Tower.
  - Ferry Building.
  - Cable cars.
  - **Sutro Tower**, in the backdrop: it went from "aesthetic horror" in 1971 to beloved icon, and it tells you where you are ([SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/vault/article/sutro-tower-san-francisco-18172001.php)).
- **Hated or contested:**
  - **Salesforce Tower**, the "Manhattanization" of San Francisco, with an "air of detachment". Its LED crown could be a beacon: towers in its view get +range at night ([SFGate](https://www.sfgate.com/local/editorspicks/article/salesforce-tower-tech-leaving-san-francisco-16053690.php), [Broke-Ass Stuart](https://brokeassstuart.com/p/why-is-the-salesforce-tower-so-hated)).
  - **Vaillancourt Fountain**, the Embarcadero's brutalist "loathsome monstrosity". Its removal was approved in 2025–26, and a fire broke out during dismantling ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/local/san-francisco/2026/01/14/vaillancourt-fountain-removal-approved-board-of-supervisors-sf), [The Art Newspaper](https://www.theartnewspaper.com/2026/05/08/vaillancourt-fountain-san-francisco-dismantling-fire)). Joke rule: a demolition site that drops debris on passing bugs.

**Skip:** anything that mocks homelessness or the drug crisis. It's the lazy SF joke, and it punches down.

## 6. Portland, Oregon (suggested: Downtown, defend Pioneer Courthouse Square)

Portland's special twist is **bridges**. The Willamette drawbridges (Broadway, Burnside, Hawthorne, Morrison) lift for river traffic, about 10 minutes each, never at rush hour ([Multnomah County via KGW](https://www.kgw.com/article/traffic/increased-bridge-lifts-could-cause-traffic-delays/283-b2c569de-c918-4eec-9eb8-67b5cfdceaeb), [OPB](https://www.opb.org/article/2024/03/02/8-hour-shifts-8-minute-bridge-lifts-at-work-with-portland-bridge-tenders/)). A lifted bridge cuts the east side off the level, a natural mechanic both ways:
- Power-up **Request a Bridge Lift**: cuts off an east-side station for 30 seconds.
- Debuff **Lift Stuck**: the Hawthorne really got stuck up in 2025 ([KPTV](https://www.kptv.com/2025/04/30/hawthorne-bridge-lift-stuck-air/)), and your reinforcements can't cross.

### Events
- **Snowpocalypse** *(bad)*: an inch of snow shuts the city down. All towers fire at half rate, everything slows. Real: February 2023 brought 11 inches, the second-snowiest day on record, and people spent the night on freeways ([KATU](https://katu.com/news/local/remembering-snowpocalypse-2016-a-year-ago-portland-metro-blanketed-by-foot-of-snow), [KGW](https://www.kgw.com/article/news/local/the-story/portland-snow-storm-response-history/283-9165b2f2-9712-4bf9-a74c-ccab2b02bacf)).
- **Permanent drizzle** *(city passive)*: everything is always Wet, which makes Portland the Tesla city once Pass 8's Cryo "wet" + Tesla synergy lands.
- **Zoobomb** *(good)*: every Sunday, riders on tiny kids' bikes bomb down from the West Hills. A downhill sweep that scatters bugs on one street; the "Zoobomb Pile" of loaner bikes is at 13th and Broadway ([Travel Portland](https://www.travelportland.com/culture/weird/), [Wandering Wheatleys](https://wanderingwheatleys.com/portland-oregon-unique-unusual-things-to-do)).
- **The Big Ride** *(mixed)*: thousands of cyclists take over the streets as a slow moving wall. The World Naked Bike Ride averages about 10,000 riders, the biggest in the world ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Portland_World_Naked_Bike_Ride)); for the game use the family-friendly version, the Providence Bridge Pedal.
- **Flaming Bagpipes** *(good)*: a masked, kilted unicyclist plays flaming bagpipes down one street, setting a fire trail. It's an homage to the Unipiper, a real local performer ([Travel Portland](https://www.travelportland.com/culture/weird/)), so ask him or keep it an original character.

### Power-ups

## 7. Guardrails before any of this ships

- **Trademarks and team IP.** Team names, nicknames and mascots (Gritty, the Phanatic, "Broad Street Bullies", Timbers), and company names and logos (Wawa, Dunkin', Citgo, Pepsi, Domino, Mister Softee, Waymo), are trademarks. Use original homages (the Orange Menace, a generic ice-cream truck, a generic robotaxi) and get legal advice before using any real mark.
- **Buildings versus sculptures.** US copyright generally allows pictures of buildings visible from public places, but **not** of sculptures and public art (*Portlandia*, *LOVE* and the *Clothespin* may all be protected), and signs on buildings are trademarks. Buildings drawn as extruded footprints are fine; hero models of sculptures and signs need a check *(this is a summary, not legal advice)*.
- **Real people.** Don't name or depict living people (players, mayors, street performers) without permission; use archetypes.
- **Tragedies and punching down.** Out: the MOVE bombing, the Molasses Flood, the Marathon bombing, transit fires, homelessness and addiction. Local memes include all of these; the game doesn't need them.
- **Tone.** Aim for "locals would laugh and share it", not "outsiders sneering". A resident's once-over per city is the cheapest insurance.
